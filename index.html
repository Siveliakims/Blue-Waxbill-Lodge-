ABOUT OUR LODGE

Hospitality inspired by the beauty of nature.

Located along the main road to South Luangwa National Park's main gate in Mfuwe, Blue Waxbill Lodge offers visitors a welcoming place to stay while exploring this remarkable part of Zambia.

Our aim is to provide comfortable accommodation, food services and convenient arrangements that make every guest's visit enjoyable.
